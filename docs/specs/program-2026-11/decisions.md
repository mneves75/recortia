| 2026-11-20 | File-save guarantees by volume class (refines the file-save threat model) | Volumes that enforce per-file owners and modes (APFS, HFS+): the full guarantee — sanitized bytes only, permissions never wider than the replaced file's as read at save time (owner-only whenever its owner or group cannot be kept), no symbolic links, nothing deleted that the save did not create, verified by moving an entry into the private staging folder before checking and removing it.
